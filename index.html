READOUT 01 / THE DIAGNOSIS

Why no one has a clean number

Over the last two years, every company was told to adopt AI, fast. Teams signed up for tools. People put ChatGPT and Claude on company cards. Software you already pay for quietly raised its price to include AI. Some bills now rise with every use.

The spend is real, but it's scattered across cards, contracts and renewals, and very little of it is labeled "AI."

That's not a failure of finance. It's how AI got bought.
